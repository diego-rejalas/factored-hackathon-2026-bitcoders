The model helps. The rules decide.

The policy is code, outside the model. The model reads the message and drafts the reply, and its second look for fraud can only add caution.

Resolves alone

A declined or reversed charge, the only candidate, under USD 500. It rereads the bank's record before it says so.

Asks first

Zero or several candidates, or a detail missing. Two rounds at most, then it escalates. It never guesses.

Hands to a person

An approved or pending charge, suspected fraud, USD 500 or more, or an unknown amount. The specialist gets the case prepared.
